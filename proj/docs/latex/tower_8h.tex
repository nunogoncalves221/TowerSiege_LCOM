\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Tower placement, upgrading, and attack logic. 

Manages all placed towers on the map. Each tower scans for enemies in range every tick and fires a bullet at the closest one. A click-\/driven popup lets the player remove or upgrade towers on blank tiles.
