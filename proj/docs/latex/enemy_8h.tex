\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Enemy entity definition and per-\/enemy lifecycle functions. 

Declares the \doxylink{structenemy__t}{enemy\+\_\+t} structure that represents a single enemy on the map, along with functions to create, update, draw, and destroy one enemy at a time. Higher-\/level coordination of multiple enemies is handled by the enemy\+\_\+manager module.
